# How to Turn Your Ebook Into 5 High-Performing Reels (With Script Templates)

**Meta Description:** Learn how to repurpose your ebook into 5 Instagram/Facebook Reels with hook-driven scripts, production specs, and posting strategy.
**Target Keyword:** ebook to reels content repurposing
**Secondary Keywords:** reel scripts from ebook, content repurposing framework, social media video scripts
**Estimated Read Time:** 8 min

You spent weeks writing an ebook. It is full of valuable insights, frameworks, and actionable advice. But if it only lives as a PDF download, you are leaving most of its value on the table.

The best content creators treat every long-form piece as a source mine. One ebook can generate dozens of short-form videos — each one driving traffic back to the original lead magnet. Here is how to do it systematically.

## Step 1: Map Your Ebook to Reel Concepts

Go through your ebook chapter by chapter. For each chapter, ask:

- What is the single most surprising or counterintuitive insight?
- What framework, checklist, or numbered list does this chapter contain?
- What myth does this chapter bust?
- What mistake does this chapter help readers avoid?

Each answer is a potential reel. A 7-chapter ebook typically yields 10-15 viable reel concepts.

### Reel Format Templates

Not every insight works as the same type of reel. Match your content to the right format:

| Content Type | Reel Format | Example Hook |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| Myths | Myth-busting (bust 3 myths in 45 sec) | "You don't need X to do Y." |
| Step-by-step process | Roadmap teaser (3 phases in 50 sec) | "Here's how to do X in 90 days." |
| Checklists or assessments | Countdown list (5 signs in 55 sec) | "Most people think they can't..." |
| Decision frameworks | Comparison (DIY vs hire in 45 sec) | "Should you X or Y?" |
| Common mistakes | Mistake reveal (tease top mistakes in 50 sec) | "Stop making mistake #3..." |

## Step 2: Write the Script With Timestamps

Every reel script needs four elements at every timestamp:

1. **Timestamp** — exact seconds for each beat
2. **Visual direction** — what the viewer sees
3. **On-screen text** — what text overlays appear
4. **Voiceover / talking head direction** — what the speaker says and how

### Script Structure

Every reel follows this arc:

- **Hook (0:00-0:04):** Bold claim, question, or curiosity gap. This determines whether someone keeps watching or scrolls. Spend the most time here.
- **Body (0:04-0:35/0:45):** Deliver 3-5 points from the ebook chapter. Each point gets 8-10 seconds. Use text overlays to reinforce key phrases.
- **Reframe (0:35-0:40):** One sentence that reframes everything the viewer just heard. This is your quotable moment.
- **CTA (0:40-0:50):** Direct, specific call to action. "Download the free playbook — link in bio." or "Take the free scorecard — link in bio."

### Hook Formula

The hook must do one of these:

- **Bold claim:** "You don't need to code to use AI."
- **Curiosity gap:** "Stop making mistake #3..."
- **Direct address:** "Here's how to implement AI in your business in 90 days."
- **Challenge assumption:** "Most founders think they're not ready for AI..."
- **Provocative question:** "Should you hire someone or figure it out yourself?"

## Step 3: Add Production Specs

For each reel, document:

- **Visual direction:** B-roll suggestions, text overlay styles, transitions between sections
- **Audio notes:** Background music mood, sound effects, volume dip instructions
- **End card template:** Consistent across all reels for brand recognition
- **Hashtag set:** 5-10 relevant hashtags per reel (mix of broad and niche)
- **Caption:** Full post caption with the hook restated, key takeaways expanded, and CTA

## Step 4: Plan Your Posting Schedule

Space your reels 2-3 days apart. A 5-reel series should take about 10 days to publish. Post schedule considerations:

- **Monday:** Bold claim or myth-busting content (high energy start to the week)
- **Wednesday:** Educational or how-to content (mid-week learning mode)
- **Friday:** Interactive or assessment content (weekend engagement)
- **Tuesday/Thursday:** Decision-making or mistake-avoidance content (work-hours focus)

Batch film all 5 reels in one session for visual consistency. Same background, same lighting, same wardrobe.

## Step 5: Build the Repurposing Chain

Each reel generates further derivatives:

- **Ad hooks:** Each reel hook becomes a paid ad variant
- **Email teaser:** Announce the series to your email list
- **Carousel posts:** Expand list-format reels into swipeable carousels
- **Quote graphics:** Pull key one-liners from each reel
- **Blog expansion:** Turn the most-engaged reel into a full blog post

## Key Takeaways

- Every ebook chapter contains 2-3 reel concepts — mine them systematically
- Match content types to reel formats (myths, countdowns, comparisons, mistake reveals)
- Script every reel with timestamps, visuals, on-screen text, and voiceover direction
- Batch film for consistency, space posts 2-3 days apart
- Each reel feeds a repurposing chain that multiplies your content output

## Next Steps

Pick your highest-value ebook chapter — the one with the most surprising insight or the most actionable framework. Write one reel script using the template above. Film it. Post it. Measure the engagement. Then do the next four.

One ebook. Five reels. Dozens of derivative pieces. That is how content ecosystems work.
